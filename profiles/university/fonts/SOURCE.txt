The University's typefaces, kept on the site so no visit asks another company for them (21 Nov 2026).

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (Braille Institute of America), Bricolage Grotesque (Mathieu Triay), Inter (Rasmus
Andersson), Fraunces (Undercase Type), Literata (TypeTogether) and Schibsted Grotesk (Schibsted, Bakken and Baeck).
Each is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows them to be used, copied and served with a site.

The files are the woff2 subsets and the stylesheets as Google Fonts served them on 21 Nov 2026, fetched once and
renamed by family and subset. inter.css, hyper.css and literata.css name only files in this folder.

unbounded-latin.woff2 (24 Nov 2026) is voidtv.net's own copy of Unbounded (NamedFont), from assets/brand/fonts, also
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. It sets only the voidtv.net wordmark in the header, the way home.
