# Hunts on Live TV (an idea to shape, nothing built)

Bryan, 20 Nov 2026: "we van have hunts by having hints and such pop up at times. this opens a whole new genre we can create".

## Why it works on voidtv.net
- Live TV is the same for everyone at the same moment, so a clue can pop up on every phone watching a channel at once,
  across a living room, a town or the world.
- The pieces are built:
  - the small VTV icon on the big screen and the pop-up on the phone (this folder);
  - questions timed to the moment of an episode (the clip chat's trivia/src/timed.js);
  - the knowledge of what plays on every channel all week (the Live TV schedule).

## Kinds of hunt
1. **Tune in to collect.** A clue says where to be: "Tonight a sheriff loses his hat. Find the channel before 9." The phone
   knows what the big screen is playing, so being on the right channel at the right time collects it. Surfing becomes the game.
2. **Spot it.** At a moment in an episode the phone asks "What color was the car that just drove by?" or "Finish the line".
   Built from the clip chat's approved scene lines, the same way the during-show trivia works now.
3. **The trail.** Each answer gives the next clue, on another channel or later in the evening. Finish the trail across a
   night or a week.
4. **Who said it.** A line pops up, and you find the show and the channel it is on before it airs.

## How it would feel
- On the big screen: only the small icon, as with trivia. On the phone: the clue, a stamp for each find, and the trail so
  far. Saved on the phone only, no account.
- Finishing a hunt gives a card to keep or share, never a prize that needs a name or an account.
- Kids: their own gentle hunts would follow the Kids rules (making things and first tries). Kids has no Live TV, so they
  would need their own shape.

## What it needs
- A clue format: the clue, when or where it pops up, and how an answer is checked. The Trivia chats could write clues from
  the catalog and the approved transcript lines.
- One new phone action, collect, which the big screen checks against what it plays at that moment.
- The pop-up as built, with a second kind beside trivia: hint.
