# VoidTV games on phones: the list

One place for every game asked for, built or thought of. Bryan, 22 Nov 2026: "all games with cards we can build from
and ahave in a choice selectpor", then "find other novel games or tricks we can do" and "why does it have to be
cards...why not bingo?"

It does not have to be cards. What is built is a shared big screen, one private thing on each phone, and a rule file
that says what may happen. A card was only the first kind of private thing.

## Built (rule files in `cards/games/`)

| Game | Players | Seen played on the pages |
|---|---|---|
| Crazy Eights | 2 to 7 | Yes, a whole game |
| Go Fish | 2 to 6 | Yes, the first asks |
| Hearts | 3 to 5 | No. Its rules are tested; its table drawing is unseen |
| Spades | 4, partners | No. The pages cannot yet show a bid button that asks a question |
| Rummy | 2 to 6 | No. The pages cannot yet show several card moves in a turn, or sets and runs in groups |
| Conquian | 2 | No. Same as Rummy |

Spades as built: bid your books, nil is 100 either way, ten bags cost 100, first side to 500, and a side that sinks
to 200 below nothing loses. No jokers. Rummy as built: sets and runs, lay off on anyone's, the card taken from the
pile cannot go straight back, first out wins the deal. Conquian as built: forty cards, the 7 sits next to the jack, a
card is never taken into the hand; not yet in it: making the other player take a card that fits their sets, and
moving cards between your own sets.

## Mind readers

His words: "can you include a few magic card type math read your mind games?" Old self-working maths tricks. The
table reads each phone's secret pick, and can show how it works afterwards.

| Trick | What you do | How the table knows |
|---|---|---|
| The 21-card trick | Tap the column your card is in, three times | Your card always ends in the middle |
| Number-guessing cards | Tap yes or no to six screens | It adds the first number of every yes |
| The 27-card trick | Name a number, then tap columns three times | Your card turns up at that number |
| The trail | Start on any card and hop by each card's number | Almost every start ends on the same card |
| The calendar square | Pick any three by three block of dates and say the total | The middle date is the total divided by nine |
| The birthday sum | Do five small sums with your birthday | The answer holds the month and the day |
| 1089 | Pick a three-figure number, turn it round, subtract, turn it round, add | It is always 1089 |
| Nim | Take 1, 2 or 3 sticks in turn; whoever takes the last loses | The table always wins until you find its secret |

## What stays the same

- The phone relay, the dealer and the privacy rule do not change for any of these.
- Each new kind of private thing needs its own face on the phone and its own table. That is a new look, so Bryan sees
  pictures before anything is built.
- Traditional games or our own. Never a copy of a commercial game's name, pieces, art or wording.
- The first players are a family at home with young kids. So: no play chips, and no speed games, where the relay's
  delay would decide who was first.
